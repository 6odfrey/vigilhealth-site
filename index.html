Vigil, a mental health wellbeing service.

At Vigil Health, we are dedicated to providing compassionate and comprehensive mental health services through our Adult Acute MH D2A (Discharge to Assess) Service. As a beacon of support, we specialize in helping individuals transition from mental health hospitals and units to home, ensuring a seamless and personalized journey towards recovery.

Our approach

Our approach is centered on the individual, empowering them to develop the necessary skills and self-assurance to live enriching lives.

Services Tailored to You:

Bridging the Gap with Care and Coordination

Our range of services includes expert assessments, personalized care planning, and seamless coordination with healthcare professionals, ensuring a holistic approach to your mental health journey. From aiding with daily activities to connecting you with valuable community resources, we stand by you every step of the way.

How to Refer:

To take the first step towards mental health and recovery, refer a patient to our Adult Acute MH D2A Service by contacting us at 07789 017899 or info@vigilhealth.org. We are here to guide you through the process and offer a helping hand on your road to well-being.
